# Carbon Vinyl Editor

**Built by rudiporsik-art and Savento.**

A standalone vinyl editor for **NFS Carbon** saves (nfscrb.xyz). Build your whole livery on PC or Mac with a live 3D preview and save it straight back into your Carbon save.

## Download

Go to **Releases** (right side of this page) and download the ZIP for your system:

- Windows: `Carbon Vinyl Editor.zip`
- Mac (M1/M2/M3/M4): `Carbon Vinyl Editor Mac (Apple Silicon).zip`
- Mac (Intel): `Carbon Vinyl Editor Mac (Intel).zip`

Unzip it and start the editor. No install needed.

## Features

- Live 3D preview of your car, plus all side views at once
- Move, size, length/width, rotate and skew for every vinyl layer
- Full colour control per layer
- Copy, paste, mirror, reorder and delete layers, also across cars
- Share designs as a code and load other people's designs 1:1
- Works with the normal 40-layer limit, plus a 200-layer test mode for the vinyl mod
- Built-in **Tour**: a tutorial that walks you through every feature step by step
- English, German and Russian

## How to use

1. On nfscrb.xyz: Saves, download your save (.zip).
2. In the editor: Load save (or drag the .zip into the window).
3. Pick a car and edit your vinyls.
4. Download save, then upload that .zip on nfscrb.xyz again.

New here? Click **Tour** (bottom right of the 3D view) for the step-by-step tutorial. The **?** button shows all controls.

## Good to know

- Always keep a backup of your original save.
- If a car has a special livery (Vinyls > Specials), set it to "None" in the game, otherwise your own layers stay hidden.
- Windows may say "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".
